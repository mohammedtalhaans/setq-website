# SetQ positioning research

Research date: 2026-10-08. Primary product sources are the user brief, `gym-tracker/README.md`, `gym-tracker/dashboard/DATA.md`, `gym-tracker/dashboard/README.md` and `setq-model/README.md`. Public sources below are category and design inputs, not SetQ customer evidence.

## Recommendation

Use the requested category, **the AI native operating system for gyms**, with a specific first promise: understand the floor and make better equipment decisions. The site should connect sensing, a sample operations workspace, a conversational review and investment planning. The stronger OS ambition works when visitors can see a coherent workflow, rather than an invented catalogue of business integrations.

Lead with **Understand your floor. Decide what comes next.** This has a useful operator outcome without implying occupancy, queues, member identification or a proven financial return.

The audience hypothesis is owners and operations leaders of equipment-intensive gyms, especially several-site groups. Confirm it through pilot conversations. No SetQ voice-of-customer material exists yet.

## Primary-source findings

### Operator economics and technology spending

[HFA 2026 Operator Expectations](https://www.healthandfitnessbusiness.org/2026-global-report/operator-expectations) reports growth expectations alongside continuing cost pressure, technology spending intentions and measured staffing expectations. This supports messaging about repeatable reviews, operational clarity and spending decisions. Survey expectations are not measured SetQ outcomes or a forecast of its market. Do not reuse industry percentages as product proof.

[HFA 2025 Fitness Industry Benchmarking Report release](https://www.healthandfitness.org/hfa-releases-2025-fitness-industry-benchmarking-report/) includes reinvestment, profitability, member dynamics, facility efficiency and staffing among operator benchmarks. These are appropriate decision dimensions. An activity sensor does not measure membership retention or EBITDA; SetQ cannot promise changes to either without separate evidence.

### Existing equipment analytics category

[EGYM official analytics documentation](https://knowledge.egym.com/en/business-suite/egym/analytics/view-your-analytics.html) describes location-level member behaviour, product usage and staff activity views. [EGYM operator resources](https://uk.egym.com/en-gb/for-operators) explicitly describe usage data for its machines and Trainer app. This validates demand for an operator view while making clear that SetQ cannot claim to be the first gym analytics platform.

[GYMetrix first-party equipment-availability case study](https://static1.gymetrix.co.uk/case_studies/The_importance_of_Equipment_Availability_to_gym_customers.pdf) combines sensor measurements with gym-floor surveys. [GYMetrix first-party lean-thinking paper](https://static1.gymetrix.co.uk/case_studies/White_Paper_Lean_Thinking_in_gyms.pdf) discusses equipment availability and floor decisions. Its conclusions belong to its own methodology; do not transplant its availability thresholds into SetQ's movement-only model. Treat the materials as historical category research, not current verified product functionality. The current company domain redirects to TrackMyGym and supplies little parseable content.

### Administration is a different product promise

[Gymflow official homepage](https://gymflow.io/) describes bookings, membership billing, access control, communications and reporting. This shows what operators may assume the phrase gym operating system includes. SetQ must establish its floor/equipment scope early and avoid fake integration logos or an implied replacement of existing payment and access systems.

### AI workflow precedent

[Anthropic's Grafana case study](https://www.anthropic.com/customers/grafana) describes natural-language exploration of observability data, summaries and workflow assistance. It supports the design pattern of a grounded assistant beside a data workspace. It supplies no evidence of SetQ's implementation, gym suitability, performance or relationship with Anthropic. Use the general interaction idea, not Grafana's delivery times or customer outcomes.

### Connected-hardware marketing patterns

[Samsara equipment tracking](https://www.samsara.com/products/equipment-tracking) connects hardware, utilisation reports and inventory decisions with concrete product views and relevant CTA placement. [Verkada camera product page](https://www.verkada.com/security-cameras/) pairs visible hardware with a coherent software experience. Borrow the clarity of hardware-to-workflow storytelling and the tangible product presentation. SetQ should not inherit their production reliability, customer proof or diagnostics claims.

## Anthropic mark and attribution

Official source: [Anthropic Trademark Guidelines](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/trademark-guidelines), effective August 1, 2024, checked 2026-10-08.

The guidelines say marks require specific permission and advance approval of materials. They specify supplied unaltered images, readability and spacing, and prohibit implying sponsorship or an unauthorised relationship. This is the official published requirement, not a claim that SetQ already has approval.

The user expressly requests an Anthropic logo at every model mention. Use authentic, unaltered attribution for the planned technology, with no partnership badge or endorsement claim. The mark remains Anthropic's trademark; the linked official guidance describes brand use. Do not email Anthropic without explicit user authorisation. Do not redraw the logo as generic text or substitute a random starburst.

Use two restrained visible mentions rather than repeatedly displaying another company's brand: hero **Designed around Claude models**, assistant **Claude assistant** with **Integration in development** and **Product preview**. Each mention includes the adjacent authentic Anthropic mark. FAQ may reference **the assistant** to avoid an unnecessary third lockup.

## Preview integrity

- Dashboard scope uses fictional site names, a fixed sample date range and a permanent Sample data label.
- A numeric activity display defines activity share as sample away-from-rest time divided by sample observed equipment time; not floor occupancy.
- A short coverage cue stays next to data. Missing scope is unavailable, not zero.
- Assistant answers are explicitly sample answers. They cite the same sample window and equipment view and recommend a review, not a guaranteed purchase.
- Suggested actions remain recommendations. A button can create a local sample review item with feedback; it cannot imply an external workflow executed.
- Demo requests open a real email draft. No fake server submission or false success notification.

## Suggested section sequence

1. Brand, concrete hero promise and early-access CTA.
2. One clear floor/equipment problem.
3. Large interactive sample dashboard with machine, period and site controls.
4. Sample assistant with useful questions, contextual answers and review actions.
5. Equipment economics, separated from measured ROI.
6. Physical sensor, fit and privacy proposition.
7. FAQ resolving compatibility, measurement, availability and cost questions.
8. Demo invitation and footer with founder contact.

Do not use a customer logo wall, awards, fake reviews or stock metrics to make the product feel established. Visualise the actual hardware proposition and let the sample workspace carry the proof of design.
