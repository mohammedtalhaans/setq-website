# SetQ illustration and motion research

Inspected 8 October 2026. Source pages were read directly through the web tool; dynamic reference previews were additionally inspected in the native Codex in-app browser. No third-party workflows, routing, hooks, or skills were installed.

## Design intent

The subject is a gym operator's floor: equipment demand, reliable operations, multiple sites and a useful daily brief. The visual signature is an exploded, isometric operating model, rising from equipment signals to floor context to a brief. The four small figures share the same camera, platform thickness, sensor puck, stroke hierarchy and cream ground. Distinctive geometry includes rack uprights, barbell plates, bench upholstery, a cable-machine weight stack, inspection sheets and linked facilities.

Palette: cream `#f5f2eb`, warm brown `#5a4232`, ink `#292720`, sage `#889775`, secondary construction lines `#a39787`. All SVG geometry is original. Sage indicates a signal or useful observation. Quiet cream surfaces hide geometry behind them.

## Components and motion

`src/components/Illustrations.jsx` exports `SensorFloorBrief` (alias `LayersDiagram`), `EquipmentIllustration`, `OperationsIllustration`, `PortfolioIllustration`, `BriefIllustration`, and decorative `SetQIcon`.

The large diagram uses a 700 × 540 viewBox. Small figures use 400 × 300. Every explanatory illustration has a unique title/description association, SVG non-scaling strokes and an optional `animate={false}` prop. Root application labels should remain readable HTML; SVG callouts are secondary and disappear at narrow widths. Small icons are decorative by default.

Motion is finite: intersection entry reveals groups over 850ms, staggered by 120ms. Fine-pointer hover lifts the pertinent sheet or layer 5px over 700ms; it returns on exit. There is no ambient timer or perpetual animation. Reduced-motion users see the complete static drawing. GSAP matchMedia reverts on preference changes and unmount; IntersectionObserver and pointer listeners are disposed.

## Official Anthropic asset provenance

`public/brand/anthropic.svg` is the actual inline wordmark from [Anthropic's official company page](https://www.anthropic.com/company), selected by its `aria-label="Anthropic"`. The source path geometry and 570 × 64 viewBox are preserved. The only rendering modification is converting `currentColor` to black for a standalone SVG. It was not redrawn or sourced from a third-party icon set.

No open-source license for this brand mark was observed. Treat it as an Anthropic trademark, not as an MIT asset. Place it only beside an accurate Claude technology reference with SetQ's early-access status. Its presence must not imply sponsorship, a customer relationship or partnership.

Inspected the diagram and all four miniature figures in the in-app browser at actual card scale. No clipped silhouette, unrelated icon or placeholder artwork was observed. The resting composition works without JavaScript. `scripts/illustrations-qa.mjs` regenerates `output/illustrations-qa.html`; its transformed output module is only a QA artifact, not an application entry point. The root application still needs its normal browser motion/accessibility check after integration.
