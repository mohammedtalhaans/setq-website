# SetQ brand

## Position

An AI native operating system for gym owners and operators, beginning with intelligence from their equipment. SetQ bridges the physical floor and the owner's next decision.

The repeated brand language is **"Your gym, one step ahead."** and **"A feel for your floor. A head for your business."** Both describe an operating advantage without inventing measured results.

## Mark

The wordmark combines a closely fitted Instrument Sans `Set` with an original Q, built from two concentric circles and a purposeful diagonal tail. The Q represents a continuing loop from equipment signal to understanding to action. The separate square Q mark is the favicon/social signature.

Use walnut on cream. Invert to cream on walnut when the surface calls for it. Keep the surrounding space at least half the mark's cap height. Do not stretch it, add glowing effects, or place it over busy equipment detail.

Secondary text uses darker warm ink where small text needs accessible contrast. Sage is primarily a signal, material, chart fill and decorative accent; a darker green carries readable small labels.

## Type

Instrument Sans Variable for the operating voice and interface. Newsreader italic only for a human accent within short display statements. IBM Plex Mono for timestamps, equipment tags and sample-data context. All three are self-hosted WOFF2; no font CDN calls.

## Materials and motion

Plaster, light timber, walnut steel, warm leather, sage mats and small brass joints define the physical world. Original diagrams use the same palette and perspective. The hardware view retains actual dimensions and topology while adapting its finish to the SetQ palette.

Motion follows a signal-to-decision rhythm: deliberate arrival, selective response, clear rest. The gym and hardware use demand rendering. SVG hover lift and section reveals are finite. Reduced motion removes unnecessary movement while preserving every control.

## Voice

Clear, considered, grounded in the owner's decisions. Say equipment, floor, team, review and next step. Explain the outcome before the mechanism. Avoid inflated intelligence claims and synthetic social proof. Early access is an invitation to help shape a useful product.

## Technology identification

The shared technology line reads **"Claude [Claude symbol] powered AI native gym operating system"**. It uses the official Claude sunburst from claude.com, preserving the original rust colour and path geometry. Preview/development status remains visible. The mark identifies technology and does not imply sponsorship, certification or a partnership.
