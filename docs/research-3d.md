# SetQ 3D visual research

Research completed 8 October 2026 before scene implementation. The three supplied Recent pages were opened and their actual preview imagery inspected in the Codex browser; official hardware brand pages were also read and visually inspected. Observations below describe the visible references, followed by original SetQ design decisions. None of their assets are reused.

## Supplied references

- [Casino Hero — Dev Patel](https://recent.design/i/dtnuyvc-casino-hero-section): quiet centered typography sits above a large roulette sculpture, surrounded by chips, dice, flames and purple foreground smoke. The visual is concentrated in one big lower-half gesture. The useful lesson is scale and scene staging, not its neon color language. SetQ reserves visual richness for the gym model and keeps surrounding UI quiet.
- [AI-Generated Macro Pad — Pietro Schirano](https://recent.design/i/jxpvlxl-ai-generated-macro-pad-3d-model): pale rounded enclosure, layered keys, translucent upper details, tiny black fasteners and seams, elevated oblique camera, and a broad soft grounding shadow. The page describes multiple angles and an exploded view. SetQ borrows the coherent manufacturing detail, carefully varied material roughness, and elevated studio camera. It does not borrow the device shape.
- [Ice Fracture Hero — Zsolt Kacso](https://recent.design/i/ld870xq-ice-fracture-hero-animation): an ice-like central sculpture overlaps the space above a large two-line headline in a dark monochrome composition. The page describes cyclic shattering/reforming with sprite alpha shards. The inspected preview establishes the sculpture/type composition; it does not establish frame-by-frame timing. SetQ uses one controlled reveal and short interaction motion instead of perpetual ambient spectacle.

## Primary hardware sites

- [Eight Sleep, Australian homepage](https://www.eightsleep.com/au/): the current Pod hero makes the ribbed physical object enormous, places a short product identity above it, and offers a single prominent next step. Its subsequent cover / hub / app explanation makes an invisible system tangible. SetQ should explain machine / sensor / floor intelligence with the same clarity. The blue backdrop and commercial claims are not imported.
- [Oura homepage](https://ouraring.com/): the inspected hero places polished gold hardware on dark textured stone in a warm neutral setting, with very large restrained typography. The contrast of small precision hardware against a natural material gives a health technology object human scale. SetQ uses leather, wood, plaster and small ivory sensors rather than cold glowing data tubes.
- [Teenage Engineering OP–1 field](https://teenage.engineering/products/op-1): restrained gray product presentation, an extremely detailed physical object, and fine technical captions accompany an extended engineering story. SetQ adopts purposeful physical details: cable pulleys, weight plate separation, equipment feet, narrow brass trim and sensor seams.
- [Nothing official homepage](https://nothing.tech/): image-led product/campaign sections and strongly branded utility typography. Its consent overlay obscured the first inspected image, so no specific unobscured hero geometry is claimed. SetQ uses consistent small utility labels rather than unrelated decoration.
- [Formlabs official homepage](https://formlabs.com/): strong industrial product hero, direct explore/contact sales choices, followed by clear printer/material/application taxonomy. Region/cookie overlays were visible during inspection. SetQ should distinguish its current strength-machine sensor proposition from the broader illustrated gym vision.

## Original scene direction

An architectural gym miniature on a rounded plaster plinth. Warm cream `#f5f2eb`, clay steel `#5a4232`, ink rubber `#292720`, sage upholstery `#889775`, light oak, small muted brass accents. An oblique orthographic camera makes a compact cutaway readable as a place. Equipment heights and silhouettes vary: dual cable station, lat pulldown, chest press, treadmills, bike, freeweight rack, bench, open mats. Low perimeter walls and a timber fin screen imply architecture without hiding the machines. A relief SetQ wordmark anchors the space.

Sensor details appear only on strength machines. They are small original representations informed by the existing `setq-model/model.js` proposed 36 × 29 × 11 mm enclosure proportions, ivory polymer, dark seam and brass mounting; they are not a validated enclosure or an exported manufacturing model. Cardio and open-floor areas illustrate the long-term floor vision, not current instrumentation claims.

Interaction selects strength / cardio / recovery (UI label: Open floor). Sage floor inlays indicate selection without glowing. A short selected strength weight-stack lift explains machine activity; zone changes also adjust a gentle camera position. Reduced motion presents the final still pose. The canvas uses demand rendering and IntersectionObserver/page visibility to stop work when hidden. The static geometries are merged by material to reduce draw calls; scene textures are generated locally, with no external model or HDR dependency.

An original single-plane fragment shader draws a quiet sage activity ring at the chest press. [The Book of Shaders chapter 5](https://thebookofshaders.com/05/) explains interpolation/shaping with `smoothstep`; [chapter 7](https://thebookofshaders.com/07/) explains circle distance fields. The shader combines distance from the UV centre, an absolute radius difference and smooth edge thresholds. Its radius expands once during the finite interaction timeline, and its opacity fades to zero. It uses normal alpha blending, no additive glow, and is suppressed under reduced motion/when hidden. It conveys an illustrative activity event, not an ultrasonic beam measurement.

## Self critique and verification

The main aesthetic risk is an entire original architectural miniature rather than a conventional dashboard mockup. The material palette is explicitly requested; specificity comes from equipment detail and original architecture. Cut decoration that does not communicate gym structure: no floating metrics in WebGL, no emissive sensor beams, no abstract orbiting particles. Parent-owned HTML controls and copy communicate the system and preserve accessible text. A visible static floor-plan fallback retains zone selection if WebGL is unavailable.

Visual QA: original scene rendered in a standalone Vite harness, inspected in the browser at desktop and 390 px viewport width. Machine silhouettes stayed readable and within the canvas. Strength, cardio and open-floor buttons updated state, and clicking the strength portion of the 3D model selected strength correctly. Three 0.186 removed PCFSoftShadowMap; implementation uses PCFShadowMap with softened radius/light shadow intensity. A R3F-owned deprecated THREE.Clock notice remains dependency-level; no scene error was observed.
