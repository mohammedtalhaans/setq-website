# SetQ: design direction before implementation

The visitor is an owner or operator of a gym. The page's job is to turn curiosity into a conversation about an early-access pilot, addressed to founder@setq.com.au. SetQ's category is an AI native operating system for gyms; its concrete wedge is intelligence from equipment activity.

## Tokens

- Chalk `#f5f2eb`: main ground, naturally warm without yellow.
- Porcelain `#fffdf8`: raised UI surfaces and highlights.
- Walnut `#594431`: brand, primary buttons, warm hardware details.
- Graphite `#292720`: body and display typography.
- Sage `#889775`: active equipment, signal and measured activity.
- Stone `#d5cec2`: rules, secondary structure and shadows.

Instrument Sans is the precise operating voice; Newsreader italic introduces a restrained human gesture in headline fragments; IBM Plex Mono is reserved for time, sample data and equipment labels. Local font assets prevent third-party font requests. A custom SetQ symbol is an open reading of a Q as a continuous equipment-to-decision loop.

## Layout and signature

The signature is an original architectural miniature gym. Recognizable physical equipment, layered warm surfaces and sensor details connect the product to the owner's world before explaining it. The hero uses an asymmetric editorial composition: oversized, short typography at left, a roomy 3D scene at right, and small contextual interface fragments. The rest of the page alternates wide immersive product demos with quieter proof-of-utility sections. No fake social-proof logo rail.

## Actual research applications

Casino Hero on Recent uses a compact clear type hierarchy above one theatrical object. SetQ adopts the dominant object, replacing spectacle with a coherent gym world. Macro Pad contributes precision in seams, rounds, oblique view and soft shadows. Ice Fracture contributes sculptural scale and overlapping depth, not literal ice. Hairline contributes diagrammatic perspective and pointer response. ULTRAGRID's Codrops case study treats 3D objects as meaningful navigation and stresses a unified material palette and optimized geometry. Isle Thorne contributes stable columns, furniture-like warmth and progressive enhancement. The Book of Shaders contributes smoothstep-based signal fields. GSAP drives coordinated reveals; React Three Fiber drives scene composition and demand rendering. Lucide supplies small interface controls from the X article's recommended resources. Fonts are from Google Fonts via Fontsource. Theatre and Pixi were researched; avoid duplicate renderers/timeline engines where Three/GSAP/SVG already cover the actual need.

## Critique before code

A cream background with a serif headline alone would be generic. The revised direction gives SetQ a recognizable gym-specific world, walnut structural details, a custom Q mark, useful interactive demos, and a clear operator vocabulary. Serif is an accent, never a complete template skin. Motion is abundant in crafted artifacts but finite and coordinated. Reduced motion keeps all content readable and usable. Do not hijack scrolling or obscure navigation with a loading screen.

## Honesty

All dashboard numbers and AI answers are illustrative. This is an early-access product website, not evidence of a deployed Claude API or certified hardware. No occupancy, queue, identity, biometric or automatic weight-selection claims. Enquiries open a prepared email; there is no fake successful submission or invented backend.
